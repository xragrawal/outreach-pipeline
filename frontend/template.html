Five stages.

Each stage answers one question and ends with a gate. A failed gate stops the run.

No hub. The stages run in a line, not around a centre. Stage 4 reports its ties instead of hiding them: __TIE_N__ companies share one score, so their order is not a ranking.

The ranking.

Capability carries 88% of the score. Delivery and buyer carry the rest.

# Company Tier Core Score Contact

Score = 0.88 × match index + 0.07 × delivery + 0.05 × buyer. Rescaled so the best row scores 100.
